Free audit widget for agencies

Put a website & email health grader on your own site. Visitors check their domain without leaving your page: a natural conversation starter for web, email and IT agencies. Free, no signup.

1. Copy the embed code

Script (recommended, resizes automatically):

<script src="https://299nhs7sjg-netizen.github.io/stackgrade/widget.js" data-agency="Your Agency Name" data-color="0f172a" async></script>

Or a plain iframe:

<iframe src="https://299nhs7sjg-netizen.github.io/stackgrade/widget/?agency=Your%20Agency%20Name" title="Website & email health check" style="width:100%;max-width:760px;height:900px;border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>

By default the widget is compact: the grade, the category scores and the top 5 problems with fixes, plus a "See full report" link to StackGrade (about 900px tall or less on desktop).

Options

  • data-agency / ?agency=: your name, shown as "by Your Agency Name" (up to 60 characters).
  • data-color / ?color=: button color as a 6-digit hex value without the #.
  • data-domain / ?d=: optional domain to grade straight away.
  • data-mode="full" / ?mode=full: show the full report inside the widget instead of the compact view.

The free widget shows "Powered by StackGrade" with a link back. Lead capture (collecting the visitor's email for you) is not available yet.

How the white-label check works (honest version)

StackGrade has no server. A white-label snippet carries your Gumroad license key (data-license) and your branding (data-config, with a checksum that catches copy/paste damage, not a real signature). On each page load the widget asks Gumroad's public license API whether the key is valid and not refunded, disputed or cancelled, and only then shows your logo and button and hides "Powered by". If the check fails, the widget falls back to the free version. Because this runs in the visitor's browser, your key is visible in your page source and a determined person could bypass it. It is a fair-use check, not DRM.

Privacy

The widget runs entirely in the visitor's browser and stores nothing. It queries public DNS, registry RDAP, Mozilla HTTP Observatory and public job boards, exactly like the main site.
